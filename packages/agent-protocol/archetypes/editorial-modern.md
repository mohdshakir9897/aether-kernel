# Archetype: Modern Literary & Investigative Editorial (`editorial-modern`)

## Target Environment
- **Operators**: Longform readers, researchers, policy analysts.
- **Physical Context**: Tablets and desktops (45-65cm viewing distance), high sustained attention.

## Typographic Grammar
- **Display Headlines**: High-contrast serifs (`"Newsreader", "Charter", "Georgia", serif`).
- **Body Text**: Optically balanced reading type with generous line height (`leading-relaxed`, `max-w-prose`, 65-75 characters per line).
- **APCA Requirement**: Minimum $|L_c| \ge 85$ for longform readability.

## Layout & Affordances
- **Density**: Spacious. Wide asymmetric margins, pull quotes anchored to the golden section.
- **Negative Space**: $40\%\text{--}60\%$ intentional negative space to prevent cognitive crowding.
