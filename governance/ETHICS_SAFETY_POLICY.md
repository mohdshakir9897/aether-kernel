# Aether Ethics & Ergonomic Safety Policy

## 1. Principles of Visual Safety
Human visual systems did not evolve to parse high-frequency synthetic visual noise or monocultural generative templates. The pervasive deployment of autonomous coding agents creates a systemic risk of cognitive exhaustion, ocular strain, and degraded decision latency in mission-critical software.

### 1.1 Ocular Strain Prevention
- **APCA Mandatory Legibility**: Any user interface generated or verified through Aether must strictly conform to Accessible Perceptual Contrast Algorithm (APCA) contrast curves ($L_c \ge 60$ for content text, $L_c \ge 75$ for fine technical copy, $L_c \ge 90$ for low-vision safety).
- **Chromatic Aberration Constraints**: The simultaneous use of high-chroma opposite-spectrum primaries (specifically high-chroma magenta/violet paired with saturated cyan/electric-blue) induces chromostereopsis and rapid ciliary muscle fatigue. Aether enforces strict chromatic budgets.

### 1.2 Cognitive Monoculture Mitigation
Generative models default to the statistical median of their training data. This convergence destroys domain-specific affordances (e.g. replacing high-density tabular ledgers in financial software with fluffy 3-column marketing cards). Aether prohibits the suppression of domain semantics for generic decorative trends.

### 1.3 Neurodivergent & Vestibular Accommodations
Interfaces must respect ambient lux and circadian cycles, eliminate unconstrained background gradient pulses or distracting ambient animations, and provide deterministic spatial anchors.

## 2. Fair-Use & Agent Alignment Standards
Agents deploying the Aether protocol agree to:
1. Subject every generated layout to pre-flight perceptual validation.
2. Refrain from injecting synthetic filler copy ("supercharge your workflow") in place of concrete domain variables.
3. Preserve the cognitive agency of the human operator at all times.
