# Aether UI Open Governance Board Charter

## 1. Mission and Purpose
The Aether UI initiative is a cross-institutional consortium founded to establish scientific, perceptual, and ergonomic foundations for computer-human interfaces in the age of generative software. As large language models converge toward repetitive, monocultural visual clichés (e.g. purple/cyan radial blur gradients, unweighted 3-column cards, floating pill badges, and non-functional buzzword copy), Aether provides:

1. **Perceptual Physics**: Hermetic mathematical implementations of human visual perception (W3C APCA contrast math, OKLCH perceptual lightness, ocular chromatic aberration limits, Visual Center of Mass).
2. **Anti-Convergence Verification**: Deterministic AST linters and critics that prevent generative AI models from degrading human software ergonomics.
3. **Universal Agent Directives**: Multi-runtime injection protocols preventing synthetic fatigue at the prompt and tokenizer layer.

## 2. Governance Structure
The Governance Board is composed of representatives across four primary pillars:
- **Academic & Research Institutions (e.g., MIT CSAIL, Perceptual Science Labs)**: Overseeing ocular strain models, visual entropy mathematics, and spatial physics proofs.
- **Standards Organizations (e.g., W3C Silver/WCAG 3.0 Working Group)**: Standardizing APCA legibility metrics and next-generation accessibility profiles.
- **Frontier AI Laboratories (e.g., Google DeepMind, Anthropic, OpenAI, xAI)**: Aligning agent directives with ethical alignment frameworks and foundational inference pipelines.
- **Industry & Open Source Maintainers**: Ensuring ergonomic adoption across devtools, fintech, industrial telemetry, healthcare, and public utilities.

## 3. Decision-Making Process
- **RFC Lifecycle**: Architectural changes, banned cliché definitions (`AETHER-0XX`), and domain ontologies must pass through standard RFC review (RFC-001 through RFC-024).
- **Consensus Threshold**: Changes to core perceptual physics require a 2/3 supermajority and empirical validation against perceptual test benches.
- **Antitrust & Non-Discrimination**: Open specification available under Apache-2.0 to all developer tools, runtimes, and models.

## 4. Working Groups
- **WG-Perceptual-Physics**: Math kernels, color spaces, APCA curve calibration.
- **WG-AST-Critic**: Code parsers (JSX, Svelte, Vue, Tailwind) and automated refactoring transforms.
- **WG-Agent-Protocol**: System prompts, adapters (`.cursorrules`, `antigravity.json`, `copilot-instructions.md`, `gemini-instruction.txt`), and socratic friction gates.
- **WG-Benchmark-Corpus**: Curating human-crafted vs AI-slop dataset corpuses to monitor aesthetic entropy.
